## The big finding: why empanelled auditors matter now

On **1 Sep 2025** CERT-In issued **"15 Elemental Cyber Defense Controls for MSMEs"** (CISG-2025-03): 45 recommendations under 15 controls. Two independent summaries (a law firm and a security firm) report it says MSMEs should get a **baseline audit by a CERT-In empanelled auditing organisation at least once a year**.

If that holds, every empanelled auditor suddenly has a very large number of small clients and needs a fast, repeatable way to gather network evidence. That's the product Vedha already has.

**Verify the exact wording in the PDF before using it in any message:** "mandatory" vs "advised" changes the pitch. Sources: [CERT-In PDF](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/Elemental_Cyber_Defense_Controls_for_MSME.pdf), [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=7f1ac82f-8d75-4cf4-84df-97355ed9dc54), [S.S. Rana & Co.](https://ssrana.in/articles/cert-in-issues-guidelines-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-in-msmes/). This also feeds #13 and #15.
