# #11 Discovery-call script (T4 + the unprompted-recall question)

20 minutes, video or phone. **Done when:** 5 calls are on the calendar (Sun 11 Oct). The goal is to learn, not to pitch: talk for 30% of the call at most.

## Before the call (2 minutes)

- Their type (MSP, auditor, ISO/CA, installer), size, and roughly how many small-office clients they serve
- Open the scoreboard sheet so you can log answers live

## The call

**0–2 min: why we're talking**
> "Thanks for the time. We're early, and we want to build Vedha around how firms like yours actually work. I'll ask more than I talk."

**2–4 min: the unprompted-recall question (ask before you describe Vedha at all)**
> "When a small-business client asks you, 'Is our office network safe?', what do you do today? What tools or names come to mind?"

Write down **every name and word they use, verbatim**. This is the Monday scoreboard's *unprompted recall*: the week someone says "Vedha" here without prompting, the brand is working.

**4–10 min: their world (T4)**
1. "Walk me through the last time a client had a security scare. What happened, and who paid for what?"
2. "What do you check today on a new client's office network? How long does it take, and do you charge for it?"
3. "Have clients asked about CERT-In's MSME controls or DPDP yet? What did they ask?"
4. "When you find something wrong, how do you show the owner, and how do you get paid to fix it?"
5. "What would stop you from recommending an outside check to a client?" (Listen for the "it exposes my work" objection; the answer is in #17.)

**10–15 min: show, don't tell**
Share the 30-second film or one real report page. Then ask:
> "What in this would your clients actually care about? What would you remove?"

**15–18 min: Partner Zero**
> "We're running the first checks with a few partners, with the founders alongside. [offer from #10]. Would you want to try one with a client in the next two weeks?"

**18–20 min: close**
- "Who else should I talk to?" (ask for one introduction)
- Agree the next step and date before hanging up
