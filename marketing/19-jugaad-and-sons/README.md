## The idea
Jugaad & Sons is a **fictional** family business whose office network has collected years of jugaad: quick fixes that became open doors. Each item is a small, affectionate story plus one fix. The humour is about habits, never about people being stupid; there's no fear, and no religious imagery (Diwali comes through as *Safai*, the cleaning).

**Every door is something Vedha's check really finds:** remote desktop exposed, an open share, an internet-exposed DVR, router admin from outside, an unauthenticated database, unknown or forgotten devices, anonymous FTP, an open printer admin page, a flat guest Wi-Fi (devices visible on one network). No backup, password-policy or patching items (owner scope).
