# #17 Objection sheet (partner kit)

Short answers a founder or partner can say out loud. Each one is grounded in what Vedha really does. **Done when:** it's in the partner kit (#23).

| They say | Answer | Proof to show |
|---|---|---|
| **"Your report exposes my work."** | "It exposes the network, not you, and every finding comes with a fix that **you** do and get paid for. The re-check then shows the owner that *you* closed it. In the margin model, remediation is worth about as much to you as the check margin." | `07-pricing/margin_model.xlsx`: the remediation vs check-margin rows |
| "My clients won't pay for security." | "They pay for proof. The report is plain language: what's open, the evidence, and the fix. It's also the evidence their auditor or insurer asks for." | A report page; the #15 one-pager |
| "We already use [scanner]." | "Keep it. Vedha is a 7-day check with a re-check and an owner-readable report, sold through you. Most scanners give you a list; we give the owner a reason to act and you the work." | The film; the report's "verified" page |
| "Is this CERT-In compliant?" | "No tool makes anyone compliant. Vedha gives network evidence for 3 of the 15 MSME controls and part of 4 more; an empanelled auditor assesses all 15." | `15-certin-guide/` one-pager |
| "Is it safe to put your agent on a client network?" | "It only dials out over one port and opens nothing new. It collects facts, not files, and the data stays the client's." | `probe/DATA_HANDLING.md`; #9 clause |
| "What if it breaks something?" | "The checks are network-level and non-disruptive by design, run in an agreed window, and you're in control of the start and stop." `[CONFIRM: the product's safe-scan guarantees in writing before using this line]` | — |
| "Clients don't want another vendor." | "There isn't one. You stay their vendor; Vedha is behind you." | The partner card (co-branded front) |
| "7 days is too long." | "It's 7 days of the agent running, not 7 days of your time. Most of your work is the fixes, which you bill." | #16 founder-hours log |
| "Why should I trust a new company?" | "Don't take our word for it: run one check with a client you know well, with the founders alongside (Partner Zero)." | The #10 offer |
| "What does it cost?" | "We're setting partner pricing with our first firms; that's part of Partner Zero." (Until #7 is signed off.) | — |

**Never say:** "secure", "protected", "guaranteed", "compliant", "certified".
