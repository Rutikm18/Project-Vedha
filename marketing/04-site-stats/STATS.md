## Replace with (pick by page)

### 1. Why it matters now: DBIR 2026 (recommended hero stat)

> Exploitation of vulnerabilities is now the most common initial access vector in breaches: **31%**.
>
> *Source: Verizon 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report.*

- The brief's stat card points to page 10 of the report. **Copy the sentence exactly from page 10 before publishing**; my wording comes from secondary coverage ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/25/lessons-from-verizon-dbir-2026-findings/), [Tenable](https://connect.tenable.com/discussions/vulnerability-watch/key-findings-from-the-verizon-dbir-2026-slower-vulnerability-remediation-meets-f/111972)). Report PDF: [verizon.com](https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/T343/reports/2026-dbir-data-breach-investigations-report.pdf).
- **Why it fits Vedha:** "find what's open" is literally finding the exposed, exploitable services. It's a fact, not a scare line.
- Supporting context (same coverage, verify before use): credential abuse fell to 13%; only 26% of CISA KEV vulnerabilities were fully fixed in 2025 (38% the year before); median time to patch rose from 32 to 43 days; over 22,000 confirmed breaches analysed across 145 countries.

### 2. Indian SMEs, in their own words: TTBS × CMR 2026

> Only **12%** of Indian SMEs continuously monitor their cybersecurity, and **4 in 10** experienced a cyber incident in the past two years.
>
> *Source: Tata Tele Business Services and CyberMedia Research, "SME Digital Insights 2026: Cybersecurity" (survey of 800 IT heads and decision-makers in 8 Indian cities).*

- Sources: [KNN India](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/msme/84-of-indian-smes-to-raise-cybersecurity-spending-as-cyber-threats-intensify-ttbs-cmr), [CIOL](https://www.ciol.com/news/indian-smes-want-to-spend-more-on-cybersecurity-readiness-is-the-bigger-problem-12546224). `[CONFIRM: release month from the TTBS press release]`
- **Best for the home page and the partner page.** It's Indian, it's about SMEs, and it's about visibility, which is exactly what Vedha gives.
- Other usable lines from the same study: 84% plan to raise cybersecurity spend in the next 12–24 months; 45% say lack of expertise is the biggest challenge (useful for partner pages: "that's what partners are for"); 35% run several tools with limited visibility of overall risk.

### 3. Cost of a breach in India: IBM 2026 (use with care)

> The average cost of a data breach in India reached **₹25.5 crore** in 2026.
>
> *Source: IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026 (India).*

- Sources: [DQ India](https://www.dqindia.com/news/india-average-data-breach-cost-hits-record-rs25-5-crore-ibm-2026-12223128), [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/ibm-report-indias-average-data-breach-cost-hits-record-inr-25-5-cr); earlier editions are on [IBM India Newsroom](https://in.newsroom.ibm.com/2025-08-07-India-Records-Highest-Average-Cost-of-a-Data-Breach-IBM).
- **Recommendation: don't use this on owner-facing pages.** IBM's sample is mostly large organisations, so ₹25.5 crore isn't what a 40-person business would lose, and it reads as a scare number (against the brand rule "never frightening"). Use it only in partner or press material, with that context stated.

## Credit line format (use everywhere)

`Source: <Publisher> <Report name> <Year>.` Put it directly under the number in the same block, not in a footer. For the web, link the report itself, not a news article, once you've checked the exact wording.
