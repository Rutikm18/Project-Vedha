# #16 Partner Zero checks #1 and #2, with founder hours logged

**Done when:** 2 debriefs are written and founder hours are recorded (Sun 18 Oct). The hours feed the #7 margin model ("Founder hours per check"), which decides whether the small tier is viable.

## Before each check (day 0)

- [ ] The partner and the client sign the check terms with the #9 data-use clause; WhatsApp opt-in recorded (#14)
- [ ] Scope written down: the client's office subnets only (no cloud), the probe host, the 7-day window
- [ ] Probe installed by the partner, with a founder on the call; it only dials out (`probe/DEPLOYMENT.md`)
- [ ] Start the hours log (`hours_log.csv`); log **every** founder minute, including messages

## During (days 1–7)

| Day | What happens | Founder role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery + inventory runs | Check that coverage looks complete; agree with the partner what "every device" means for this office |
| 2–4 | Full assessment; findings appear | Review findings with the partner: confirmed vs suspected, plain-language titles |
| 5 | Partner fixes what they can | Answer questions; **don't do the fix yourself** (we're measuring the partner-led model) |
| 6 | Re-check | Confirm closed findings show as verified |
| 7 | Report to the owner, led by the partner | Sit in; note every question the owner asks |

## Debrief (within 48 hours), one per check: `debrief_check_1.md`, `debrief_check_2.md`

1. Office profile: people, devices found, sector, partner type
2. Findings: count by severity; which 3 the owner cared about most, **in their words**
3. Fixes: what the partner fixed, hours, and whether they charged for them (the #7 remediation assumption)
4. Re-check: what verified, what didn't, and why
5. Owner's reaction to the report: what they understood, what confused them
6. Partner's verdict: would they sell this? At what price? What's missing?
7. **Founder hours, by activity** (from the log) vs the #7 assumption of 6 hours
8. One product fix, and one marketing line we learned (feed into the objection sheet, #17)

## Privacy

Debriefs never contain the client's name, IPs, hostnames or screenshots of findings. Use "Client A, 25-person trading office, Pune".
