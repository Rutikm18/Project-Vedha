CERT-In's 15 controls for MSMEs, accurately

In September 2025 CERT-In published 15 "elemental" cyber defence controls for MSMEs. Here's what they are, what they ask for, and exactly which ones a Vedha check gives you evidence for, including the ones it doesn't.

What CERT-In published

  • CISG-2025-03, dated 1 September 2025: 15 Elemental Cyber Defense Controls for MSMEs.
  • 15 controls, broken into 45 baseline recommendations.
  • It asks MSMEs to have a baseline audit by a CERT-In empanelled auditing organisation against these controls. Read the exact wording in the official PDF on cert-in.org.in.

The 15 controls, and what Vedha shows

Vedha gives network evidence for 3 controls and part of 4 more. The other 8 need other work, and that's normal: no single tool covers all 15.

What this means for you

  1. A Vedha check is not the CERT-In audit. Only an empanelled auditor can do that. Vedha gives you and your auditor evidence: what's on the network, what's open, the proof, and a re-check after fixes.
  2. Start where the risk is visible. Asset management, configurations and vulnerability assessment are where a network check gives the most, fastest.
  3. Plan the rest with your IT partner. Training, incident response, logging, backups, passwords, patching and physical security need their own work.

Common questions

Does Vedha make us CERT-In compliant?
No. Nobody honest can promise that from a network check. Vedha gives evidence for some controls; an empanelled auditor assesses all of them.

Does it cover cloud, email or laptops away from the office?
No. Office networks only.

Who does the check?
Your IT partner, using Vedha, over 7 days.
