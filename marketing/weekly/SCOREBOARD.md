## Tuesday: Signal/Noise (2 h)
One verified signal (credited source), one piece of noise to ignore, one action for an office this week. Same format as #5, in three channels.

## Wednesday: founder post or Day-N log (1 h)
What happened, what we learned, one honest number. No hype.

## Thursday: partner day (4 h)
10 conversations from the #3 list; log them in `prospects.csv`.

## Friday: ship + retro (2 h)
Ship the week's asset. Three retro lines: what worked, what didn't, what changes.
**Kill or keep:** anything that missed its target by half, twice in a row, is stopped.
