# #18 15 Controls Awareness Poll (run through partners)

**Goal:** a real, citable number for the press (#27) and the Open Door Report (#29): how many Indian small offices know about CERT-In's MSME controls. **Done when:** the poll is running (Wed 21 Oct).

## Questions (Google Form or Tally; 60 seconds; anonymous)

1. How many people work in your office? (1–10 · 11–50 · 51–200 · 200+)
2. Which city? (free text)
3. Who looks after your office network? (In-house IT person · An outside IT firm · Nobody in particular · Not sure)
4. **Before today, had you heard of CERT-In's "15 Elemental Cyber Defense Controls" for MSMEs (September 2025)?** (Yes, and we've acted on them · Yes, but haven't acted · No · Not sure)
5. **Do you know what on your office network can be reached from outside?** (Yes, we've checked · Not sure · No)
6. Has your network been checked by anyone in the last 12 months? (Yes · No · Not sure)
7. Optional: anything you'd like to know about your office network? (free text)

**Consent line (top of the form):** "Anonymous. Answers are used only for aggregated statistics in Vedha research. We don't collect names, numbers or emails." Don't add an email field.

## Distribution

- Partners forward it to their clients (the WhatsApp version below); each partner gets their own link (`?src=<partner>`) so we can thank them, without identifying respondents
- The WhatsApp channel (#14) poll uses Q5 only (native polls)
- Target: **at least 200 responses** before publishing; below that, publish as "early signals", not percentages
