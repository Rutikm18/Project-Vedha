# #28 Vedha Village kickoff and the first Open Door Clinic

**Done when:** both are held (Fri 13 Nov).

## Kickoff (45 min, video call, Village members)

| Min | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Why the Village exists; the 4 rules | Founder |
| 5–15 | Round: name, what you do, one network question you have | Everyone (90 s each) |
| 15–30 | What we learned from the Partner Zero checks (#16): 3 real, anonymised findings and their fixes | Rutik Mangale (CTO) |
| 30–40 | Pick the clinic topic by vote | Everyone |
| 40–45 | What's next: monthly clinic, the Open Door Report (#29) | Founder |

## Open Door Clinic #1 (60 min, open to Village + guests)

**Format:** one real (anonymised) office network, walked through live: what was found, the evidence, the fix, the re-check.

| Min | What |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | What a clinic is; ground rules (no client names, no IPs) |
| 5–25 | Case walk-through: "The accounts PC with remote login left open" |
| 25–45 | Members bring questions (submitted in the group beforehand) |
| 45–55 | One fix everyone can do this week |
| 55–60 | Next clinic date; the poll link (#18) |

## Before

- [ ] Case consent: the client (via their partner) agrees, in writing, to an anonymised walk-through
- [ ] Slides: 6 or fewer, built on the film's look (seal, lattice, blue/teal rules)
- [ ] Recording only with everyone's explicit OK at the start

## After

Three-line summary in the group; one Signal/Noise item from the clinic; log attendance on the Monday scoreboard.
