## Rules for partners (page 1 of the kit)

1. You keep the client. Vedha never contacts your clients directly.
2. Never say "secure", "compliant", "certified" or "guaranteed". Say "checked, found, proved, fixed, verified".
3. Never share a client's name, IPs or screenshots outside the engagement.
4. Use the co-branded assets as they are; ask us for changes.

## Testing with 3 partners

Give the folder to 3 Partner Zero partners and ask them to (a) explain Vedha to a colleague using only the kit, and (b) set up one client conversation. Note every question they had to ask us; each one becomes a kit fix.
