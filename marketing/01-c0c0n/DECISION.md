## The case for

- **Partner prospects are in the room.** Task #3 targets CERT-In empanelled auditors and ISO/CA consultants, and they're more likely at a CERT-In-adjacent, police-run conference than anywhere else this month.
- **Credibility by proximity.** Being seen at a government-organised security conference supports the CERT-In 15-controls story (#13, #15).
- **It's in South India,** where many CERT-In empanelled auditors are based.

## The case against

- **The wrong buyer.** The people who buy Vedha are growing-business owners and their local IT partners. Most c0c0n attendees are practitioners and researchers, not either of those.
- **The P0 week is already full.** There are 12 P0 tasks between 5 and 11 Oct; travel costs at least two founder-days in that week.
- **Three days' notice.** That's too late for a booth or a speaking slot, so the only option is walking the floor.

## Recommendation: go **only if all three** are true; otherwise skip

1. **One founder, Saturday 10 Oct only** (or Friday), with no booth. The other founder keeps the P0 week moving.
2. **At least 8 meetings booked before travelling**, with CERT-In empanelled auditors or ISO/CA consultants from the #3 list who've confirmed they're attending. Use the Partner Zero message (#10) to book them.
3. **Total cost (pass + travel + stay) is under the value of one partner.** If your Partner Zero margin model (#7) says one partner is worth less than the trip, skip.

**If you skip:** spend the same two days on #10 and #11 (10 partner messages, 5 discovery calls). That's more partner conversations per rupee, and it's this week's scoreboard.

**If you go:** take 100 Partner Zero QR cards (`partner-zero-card.png` / `.pdf` in this folder) and log every conversation in the #3 sheet that evening.
