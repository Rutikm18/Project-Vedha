# #21 Vedha Village: recruit 15 members (10 must be strangers)

**What it is:** a small WhatsApp group of IT partners and owners who swap real questions about office networks, and who see Vedha's work early. **Done when:** 15 people are in the group, and at least 10 didn't know a founder before (Sun 25 Oct).

## Who

- 6 IT partners (from the #3 list and Partner Zero conversations)
- 6 owners or office managers (poll respondents who asked a question in Q7, Signal/Noise readers who reply)
- 3 practitioners (auditors, ISO consultants)
- **Strangers rule:** at least 10 of 15 found us, not the other way round. That's the test of whether the brand travels.

## Invitation (DM or WhatsApp, after a real interaction)

```
Hi {first}, you asked a good question about {topic}. We're starting a small group (15 people) of IT partners and office owners who share what actually works on office networks. No selling, a few messages a week, founders answer questions. Want in?
```

## Group rules (pin these)

1. Real questions, real answers. No forwards, no promotions (including ours).
2. Never share a client's name, IP or screenshot.
3. Founders answer within a day.
4. Leave anytime; no hard feelings.

**Group settings:** only admins can edit group info; members can send. Description = the rules. Opt-in is implicit (they accepted the invite), but note each join in `14-whatsapp-channel/consent_log.csv` with purpose "Vedha Village".

## Scoreboard

Members (strangers / known), questions asked per week, questions answered by members (not founders).
